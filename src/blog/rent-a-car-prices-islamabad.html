What changes the price

  • The car. A hatchback costs a fraction of an SUV, and luxury cars cost more again. Pick the smallest car that fits your group and your roads.
  • With driver or self drive. Ask whether the rate includes a driver. With a driver, long days and overtime add to the total. Self drive needs a refundable security deposit.
  • Fuel. Check whether fuel is included. If it isn't, you either return the car with the same fuel level or pay for what you used.
  • Distance. Some rates include a daily mileage allowance, with extra kilometres charged per km. See what "200 km per day" means.
  • Out-of-city trips. Trips to Murree, Lahore or the northern areas add days and, with a driver, an out-of-city allowance for the driver.
  • How long you book. Weekly and monthly bookings are priced separately from single days. Ask for a long-term quote if you need a car for more than a few days.
  • The season. Demand peaks in the winter wedding season and around Eid, so popular cars are booked early.

The cheapest way to rent a car in Islamabad

Choose an economy car such as the Suzuki Alto for city driving, book as early as you can in busy seasons, and ask for a weekly or monthly rate if you need the car for a while. For a group, one Grand Cabin is often cheaper than two or three cars.
